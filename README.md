A mod for [Gen1Recomp](https://github.com/bryanthaboi/gen1recomp): Red, Blue and Yellow, plus
Pokémon Gold in beta.

> [!NOTE]
> **A fork of [Wilds of Kanto](https://github.com/YoDrehDenSwagAuf/overworld-spawn-mod)** by
> YoDrehDenSwagAuf and collaborators, continued from v2.2.0 as Wilds of Kanto Revival (mod id
> `wilds_of_kanto_gen3`). Install one or the other, not both.

## ✨ Features

### Core Mechanics

- 🦌 **Wild Pokémon in the overworld:** Grass, caves and water fill with visible Pokémon that
  idle and roam. Walk into one to battle it. Cave Pokémon only appear where you can actually walk.
- 🎲 **Classic encounters, your call:** Keep the original step-based encounters in grass, caves
  and on Surf alongside the visible ones, or only meet what you can see.
- ✨ **Shinies on the overworld:** A shiny shows its colours before the battle, sparkles when it
  starts, stays shiny when caught and follows you as a shiny. Choose how often they appear.
- 🗺️ **Modern Spawns ready:** With [Modern Spawns](https://github.com/poooooby/g1r_modern_spawns),
  visible Pokémon, town Pokémon and Super Rod spots use its generated tables, rare legendaries
  included.

### Your Party

- 🐕 **Followers:** Your lead Pokémon walks behind you, and up to six more can trail along in
  party order. They tuck in close while you Bike or Fly, and reappear right behind you once
  you're back on foot.
- 🌊 **Swimming and floating:** Followers and water Pokémon switch to swimming or levitating art
  on the water.
- ⚡ **Yellow's Pikachu:** Dress your starter Pikachu in one of 12 costumes, from caps to Pop Star.
- 💬 **They talk back:** Followers and town Pokémon speak with PMDCollab portraits.

### A Living World

- 🏘️ **Town Pokémon:** Peaceful Pokémon wander safe towns and buildings. They never start a
  battle or block your way.
- 🎨 **Two sprite styles:** **Poké Followers / GSC** (classic one-tile sprites, 667 species) or
  **HGSS / PokeMMO** at true size, covering all 1025 species and regional forms.
- 🌾 **Grass and silhouettes:** Pokémon sink into tall grass like trainers do. Optional
  silhouettes hide species you haven't caught, as dark shapes on land and in the water.
- 🦁 **Safari Zone:** Wild Pokémon there notice you and flee.
- 🐾 **Familiar faces:** The Pokémon-themed NPCs around Kanto — the Fuchsia City pens, the
  Pokémon Fan Club's pets, and other named Pokémon in cities and buildings — show their real
  species art instead of a stand-in sprite.

## ⚙️ Options

Open **START → OPTIONS → Wilds of Kanto Revival**.

| Option | Choices | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Show Wild Mons** | On / Off | On | Visible wild Pokémon in encounter areas. |
| **Sprite Style** | Poke Followers / GSC, HGSS / PokeMMO | Poke Followers / GSC | The look of wild Pokémon and followers (GSC classic size, HGSS true size). |
| **Pika Follower** | 13 costumes | Default | Yellow's starter Pikachu's costume. |
| **Followers** | 0-6 | 1 | Extra party Pokémon trailing your leader. |
| **Classic Enc** | On / Off | On | The original step-based encounters in grass, caves and water. |
| **Shiny Rate** | Off, 1/8192, 1/4096, 1/1024, 1/512, 1/100, 1/10, Always | 1/4096 | How often a wild Pokémon is shiny. |
| **Town Pokémon** | On / Off | On | Peaceful Pokémon in towns and buildings. |
| **Grass View** | Above / Immersed | Immersed | Draw wild Pokémon above tall grass, or partly hidden in it. |
| **Silhouette** | Off / Undiscovered / All | Off | Silhouettes for uncaught species, or for all of them. |

## 💾 Installation

1. Download `wilds-of-kanto-v*.zip` from the
   [Releases](https://github.com/poooooby/wilds-of-kanto-gen-3/releases) page (not the
   "Source code" ZIP).
2. Import it with the Gen1Recomp Mod Manager, or drop it in your mods folder.
3. Enable **Wilds of Kanto Revival**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Pokémon Gold is in beta.** If something behaves differently from Red / Blue / Yellow, please
> report it with the map, sprite style, follower count and steps to reproduce.

## 🔗 Works With

- [Modern Spawns](https://github.com/poooooby/g1r_modern_spawns) and
  [G1R National Dex](https://github.com/sanjinpepic/gen1recomp-national-dex): wild Pokémon through
  Generation 9. Add [Gen9 Dex](https://github.com/tectorifter/Gen9Dex) and
  [G9 Battle Sprites](https://github.com/tectorifter/g9-battle-sprites/) for battles, and
  optionally [G9 Battle Forms](https://github.com/sanjinpepic/gen1recomp-battle-forms).
- [Kanto Reforged](https://github.com/1Jamie/Kanto-Reforged): Generation 3 species.
- Voxel mods: Battle Art (Gen 1 and 2), Potato, Dramaless Shape and Terrarium.
- The separate **Shiny Pokémon** mod can't run alongside this one; the built-in Shiny Rate
  replaces it.
